Drag the figure to turn it. Draw a knot to sketch your own; tap a number to flip it.

Fig. 1 Trefoil

  1. Draw. Press Draw a knot, then drag one loop that crosses itself. Let go and the ends join.
  2. Choose. Tap a numbered crossing to swap which strand goes over. Crossings start out alternating.
  3. Turn and relax. Drag the figure (or use the arrow keys) to turn it. Relax lets the rope settle without passing through itself.

    Colophon The rope is drawn with three.js; everything is computed on this page in TypeScript. The Alexander polynomial is computed with exact integer arithmetic; finding the crossings uses floating point with safeguards (tolerances, plus a tiny turn of the view when a corner lands exactly on another strand). The idle sway is decoration: the analysis always uses the still view. Knot table: D. Rolfsen, Knots and Links (1976), Appendix C, via the Knot Atlas; see also: C. Livingston and A. H. Moore, KnotInfo: Table of Knot Invariants. Third-party notices. Built by Ramen Protocol with AI assistance (Claude).
